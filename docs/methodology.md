# Scoring & Analysis Methodology

## Transparent Stock Composite Score (0-100)

The composite stock score is calculated as a weighted average across six transparent components:

1. **Technical Trend Score (25%)**: Evaluates close vs 20, 50, and 200 SMAs.
2. **Momentum Score (25%)**: Evaluates RSI(14) level and MACD histogram sign.
3. **Volume Score (15%)**: Evaluates relative volume vs 20 SMA.
4. **Relative Strength Score (15%)**: Evaluates single-day performance vs benchmark.
5. **Market Regime Score (10%)**: Broad market health score (0-100).
6. **Fundamental Score (10%)**: Evaluation of P/E, ROE, profit margin, and debt-to-equity.

### Composite Score Labels:
- **80–100**: Very Strong
- **65–79**: Strong
- **50–64**: Neutral
- **35–49**: Weak
- **0–34**: Very Weak
